GPS battery display policy, 2026-10-14

User-requested labels: 정상 / 저전압 / 전압 매우 부족.
Normal >=3300mV; low >=2900 and <3300mV; critical <2900mV.
These are display/communication guidance levels, NOT battery safety cutoffs.

Source: terminal 520211, MEGACANON x2 parallel, GDS-1052-U CH1 x10,
2026-10-12 10:37 through 2026-10-14 12:44, sleep-disabled battery-98bf4a637c.
36,084 scope snapshots (160ms every 5s), 10,549 server records.
At approximately 2.85V physical / 2.81V ADC, >30s reception gaps increased.
At approximately 2.77V physical / 2.69V ADC, median successful-reception gap
was 32s, maximum 68s (formerly median 16.5s). HTTP codes were not captured.
Battery chemistry/model/capacity and exact USB removal timestamp unverified.

The initial voltage-to-percent curve is a provisional 1S GPS product estimate,
guided by the observed discharge trajectory. Rounded 5-minute physical-voltage
medians at relative elapsed positions between 10/12 10:45 and 10/14 12:00:
95% 4.066V, 90% 4.031V, 80% 3.953V, 75% 3.908V, 60% 3.767V,
50% 3.666V, 40% 3.514V, 25% 3.395V, 20% 3.400V, 15% 3.289V,
10% 3.155V, 5% 2.973V, endpoint 2.817V.
The 20-25% local recovery is merged into a monotonic 23%/3.400V anchor.
Full display is anchored at 4.200V; empty display at 2.800V. Endpoints are
display conventions: the cell is not electrochemically empty at displayed 0%.
The experiment remains ongoing. Relative run time under this load does NOT
establish true charge capacity or remaining runtime under other conditions.
Frontend and FCM explicitly call the result a voltage-based estimate.
Do not use this curve for 2S packs, smartphone batteries, or a safety cutoff.

User designated unit 520211 as the product standard on 2026-10-14.
The product now uses the piecewise ADC-to-physical voltage table in policy JSON.
Source provenance, bin counts and same-discharge held-out minute residuals are
in battery-calibration-evidence.json, reproducible with ops/build_battery_calibration.py.
2,943 paired one-minute means; initial 23 minutes excluded, exact USB removal
still unknown. Median offsets by ADC range are linearly interpolated and rounded
to 1mV (calculation resolution, NOT instrument accuracy). Out-of-range values
use the nearest endpoint offset and are marked as outside-range estimates.
Measured anchor range is raw ADC 2689..4119mV; no extrapolated slope is used.
Adjacent held-out even minutes: mean absolute residual 14.84 -> 5.08mV,
p95 absolute residual 37.97 -> 12.79mV. These are SAME discharge validation,
not independent hardware accuracy; the scope sample quantum is approximately
20mV and scope/ADC acquisition is not synchronized. Charging, USB power,
temperature/load changes and unit-to-unit tolerances remain unvalidated.

Raw vbat_mv and modem cbc_mv remain unchanged and separate in storage/API/WS.
The frontend and backend transform raw ADC exactly once at interpretation time.
Status, estimated percentage, alert eligibility and push text all use calibrated
voltage. Technical details retain original ADC and modem rail readings.
The standard is provisional and versioned while discharge measurement continues.

The same battery-policy.json feeds JS and Rust. Existing per-account push
thresholds and the 24-hour debounce are retained. A custom alert above 3.30V
uses the neutral title 배터리 알림 기준 도달 instead of falsely claiming low voltage.
The three status labels share the UI thresholds, independent of user-defined
early notification thresholds. Missing/invalid samples show 전압 미확인, not 0%.

REST includes last_battery_at and the latest row even when ADC is missing.
WS updates device detail fields and meta together, including no-GPS reports.
Battery receipt freshness is independent of the GPS fix watermark; delayed
GPS fixes may update the map but cannot restore an older battery reading.
Historical point popups keep historical battery values.
